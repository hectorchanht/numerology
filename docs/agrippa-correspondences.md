## Doctrine in one paragraph

Book I (Natural Magic) holds that each planet stamps its nature on things below it. A thing "belongs" to a planet by resemblance in colour, smell, shape, motion or effect (the doctrine of signatures). Most things take influence from several stars at once. The practical uses are choosing materials (stones, herbs, fumes), places and colours that match the planet you are working with.
