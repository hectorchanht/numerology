## Geomancy: the 16 figures

Four rows of one or two points give 2⁴ = 16 figures. Each of the seven planets owns two figures; the Moon's nodes take the last two. Rows are listed top to bottom.

The book gives three triplicity (element) schemes and prefers the one by planetary nature:

- **Fire**: Fortuna Major, Rubeus, Puer, Amissio
- **Air**: Fortuna Minor, Puella, Laetitia, Conjunctio
- **Water**: Acquisitio, Cauda Draconis, Via, Populus
- **Earth**: Carcer, Tristitia, Albus, Caput Draconis

## Heptameron: days and planetary hours

Day and night are each split into 12 unequal hours. The first day-hour belongs to the day's planet; each later hour (day then night, continuing) takes the next planet in Chaldean order.

| Day | Planet | Angels | King of the air | Heaven |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Sun | Michael, Dardiel, Huratapal | Varcan | 4 |
| Monday | Moon | Gabriel, Michael, Samael | Arcan | 1 (Shamain) |
| Tuesday | Mars | Samael, Satael, Amabiel | — | 5 |
| Wednesday | Mercury | Raphael, Miel, Seraphiel | Mediat (Modiat) | 2 (Raquie) |
| Thursday | Jupiter | Sachiel, Castiel, Asasiel | Suth | 6 |
| Friday | Venus | Anael, Rachiel, Sachiel | Sarabotes | 3 |
| Saturday | Saturn | Cassiel, Machatan, Uriel | Maymon | — |

Hour names, day: Yayn, Janor, Nasnia, Salla, Sadedali, Thamur, Ourer, Tanic, Neron, Jayon, Abay, Natalon.  
Hour names, night: Beron, Barol, Thanu, Athir, Mathon, Rana, Netos, Tafrac, Sassur, Aglo, Calerna, Salam.

## Arbatel: the seven Olympic spirits

Each governs 7 fewer provinces than the one before, 196 in all, and each rules for 490 years in turn.

| Spirit | Planet | Provinces |
|---|---|---:|
| Aratron | Saturn | 49 |
| Bethor | Jupiter | 42 |
| Phaleg | Mars | 35 |
| Och | Sun | 28 |
| Hagith | Venus | 21 |
| Ophiel | Mercury | 14 |
| Phul | Moon | 7 |

1655 prints XXXII (32) and a total of 186; the 7-step series and the total 196 require 42. Aphorism 17: Bethor began 60 BC; Och ruled until AD 1410; Hagith until AD 1900.
